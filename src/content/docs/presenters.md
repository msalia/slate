# Presenters

Presenters are the speakers, panelists, and facilitators at your events. Slate maintains a single roster of presenters across all your events.

## Your Presenter Roster

The [Presenters page](/presenters) shows all presenters you've added to your account in a searchable table. Each presenter has:

- **Name** — Their display name, shown on session cards and the attendee view.
- **Role** — Their title or position (e.g., "Engineering Lead", "CTO"). Optional but recommended for context.
- **Bio** — A short biography displayed when attendees view session details. Keep it to 2-3 sentences.
- **Events** — How many of your events this presenter is assigned to.
- **Sessions** — Total number of sessions across all events.

## Adding Presenters

There are two ways to add a presenter:

- **From the Presenters page** — Click **Add Presenter** in the top-right. Fill in their name, role, and bio.
- **From the calendar editor** — In the right sidebar, scroll to the Presenters section and click **Add presenter**. This is useful when you're building the schedule and realize you need a new presenter.

## Editing & Deleting

In the Presenters table, each row has edit (pencil) and delete (trash) buttons. Clicking edit opens a dialog where you can update the name, role, and bio. Deleting a presenter removes them from all sessions they're assigned to.

## Reusing Across Events

Presenters are scoped to your account, not to a single event. Once you add a presenter, they appear in the roster for every event you create. This means you only need to enter their details once — if a presenter speaks at multiple events, they're already in the system.

## Presenter Avatars

Slate generates avatar initials from each presenter's name. These appear as circular badges on session cards, event cards (as a face pile), and in the editor sidebar. The avatars help you visually identify who's presenting at a glance.
