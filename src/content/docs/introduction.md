# Introduction

Slate is an event schedule management tool designed for organizers who need to plan, build, and share schedules for single or multi-day events.

## What is Slate?

Whether you're organizing a tech conference, a workshop series, or a multi-day summit, Slate gives you a visual calendar editor to build your schedule, manage your presenters, and share the final agenda with attendees — all from one place.

## Key Features

- **Visual Schedule Builder** — A drag-and-drop calendar grid where you create and arrange sessions across tracks and time slots.
- **Track Management** — Organize your event into parallel tracks (e.g., Main Stage, Workshop Room) that appear as columns in the calendar.
- **Category Color-Coding** — Tag sessions with categories like Talk, Workshop, or Panel, each with its own color for quick visual identification.
- **Presenter Roster** — Maintain a reusable roster of presenters with names, roles, and bios. Assign one or more presenters to any session.
- **Draft & Publish** — Work on your schedule in draft mode, then publish it when you're ready. Attendees access the schedule via a shareable link.
- **Attendee View** — A clean, read-only view of the published schedule with filtering by track, category, and presenter.
- **Export** — Download the full schedule or individual sessions as .ics calendar files.
